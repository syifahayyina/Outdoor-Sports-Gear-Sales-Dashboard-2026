# Outdoor-Sports-Gear-Sales-Dashboard-2026

## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive outdoor and sports gear sales dashboard built in Excel using pivot tables, pivot charts, and slicers. It analyzes sales transactions across Europe from January to December 2026 to uncover insights into revenue and profitability trends, product category and product performance, country contribution, and customer types. The goal is to help business stakeholders understand what drives revenue and gross profit, and where opportunities lie.

## Dataset Description
The dataset contains sales transactions from January to December 2026, with amounts in USD. Each record represents one sales transaction.

* Order : ID, Order Date, Year, Quarter, MonthNo, Month
* Product : Product Category, Product
* Customer and location : Customer Type, Sales Channel, Region, Country
* Metrics : Units Sold, Unit Price, Discount %, Unit Cost, Revenue, COGS, Gross Profit, Gross Margin %

## Project Objectives
1. Track monthly revenue and gross profit trends and identify the peak and lowest months
2. Summarize overall performance: total revenue, gross profit, gross margin, and units sold
3. Identify the product categories and products that contribute the most revenue
4. Find categories with high revenue but low margin, and vice versa
5. Evaluate revenue contribution by country
6. Compare revenue contribution across customer types

## Visualizations Included
* KPI Summary : Total revenue, gross profit, gross margin, and units sold
* Line Chart : Monthly revenue and gross profit
* Combo Chart : Revenue and gross margin by product category
* Bar Chart : Top 5 products by revenue and top 10 countries by revenue
* Pie Chart : Revenue share by customer type
* Interactive Filters : Filter by order date, region, sales channel, and customer type

## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel : Data cleaning, pivot tables, pivot charts, and interactive dashboard
GitHub : Documentation

## Key Insights
1. Monthly revenue is lowest in February and highest in June. It declines in the second half of the year, with a small increase in November. Gross profit follows the same trend.
2. Camping and Hiking are the top revenue categories, but they have the lowest gross margins. Fitness has the lowest revenue but the highest margin. The margin gap between categories is small, about 8 percentage points.
3. The top 5 products contribute about 47% of total revenue, led by backpacks, tents, and boots.
4. The United Kingdom and Germany are the largest markets by revenue.
5. Revenue is spread fairly evenly across customer types.
